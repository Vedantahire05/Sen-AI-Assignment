# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window
- Full refunds available within **14 days** of purchase
- Submit to support@company.com
- Processed in 5–10 business days to original payment method

## After 14 Days — Credits Only
No cash refunds after 14 days unless:
1. Approved by Support Manager or above
2. Documented platform failure caused material business impact
3. Customer was charged in error

All other post-14-day cases: **account credits only**.

## Exception Process
1. Email support@company.com — subject: "Refund Exception Request"
2. Include: account email, amount, reason, evidence
3. Reviewed within 3 business days
4. Escalation path: VP of Customer Success

## Credits vs Refunds
| Scenario                        | Resolution        |
|---------------------------------|-------------------|
| Within 14 days, any reason      | Full cash refund  |
| After 14 days, SLA breach       | Credit + apology  |
| After 14 days, dissatisfied     | Credits only      |
| Billing error (any time)        | Full cash refund  |
| Charged for cancelled plan      | Full cash refund  |
| Annual plan, within 14 days     | Full refund       |
| Annual plan, after 14 days      | Pro-rata credits  |

## Churn Retention Playbook

### Tier 1 — Soft Signal (dissatisfied, no explicit threat)
- Assign to Customer Success Manager
- Offer: 1-month service credit + check-in call
- Contact within **4 business hours**

### Tier 2 — Churn Threat (explicit cancellation intent)
- Escalate to Senior CSM
- Offer one of: 2-month credit, 20% discount for 3 months, or specialist onboarding
- Contact within **2 business hours**

### Tier 3 — High-Risk Churn (VIP or public review threat)
- Immediately escalate to VP of Customer Success
- Mandatory executive outreach within **1 business hour**
- Offer: up to 3 months free or 30% ongoing discount
- Notify PR team; check current G2/Trustpilot scores
- Do NOT commit to refund without VP approval

### Tier 4 — Public Damage Threat (social media, press, review sites)
- Escalate to VP Customer Success + Head of Marketing simultaneously
- All written communications reviewed by legal before sending
- Offer: maximum retention package + CEO/VP personal apology
- Do not admit fault in writing

## Chatbot Misinformation Cases
If a customer received incorrect info from our AI chatbot:
1. Do NOT claim the chatbot gave legally binding advice
2. Acknowledge frustration with empathy
3. Retrieve actual policy and cite it clearly
4. Escalate to human with context: what chatbot said vs. actual policy
5. If financial harm occurred due to incorrect advice → escalate to legal immediately
6. Approved holding response: *"We take accuracy of our automated systems seriously. A team member will review your case and respond within [timeframe]."*

## Refund Denial Communication
- Always acknowledge frustration first
- Cite the specific policy section
- Offer best available alternative
- Never say "that's just our policy" without explanation
- Provide escalation path if customer remains unsatisfied
