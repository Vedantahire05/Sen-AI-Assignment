# Compliance FAQ

## GDPR

### Data Subject Rights (Article 15–22)
Customers and their end-users may exercise the following rights:
- **Article 15** — Right of access: receive a copy of all personal data held
- **Article 16** — Right to rectification: correct inaccurate data
- **Article 17** — Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- **Article 20** — Right to data portability: receive data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)
- **Article 21** — Right to object to processing

### Data Portability Requests (Article 20)
- Must be fulfilled within **30 days** of receipt (statutory deadline)
- Acknowledgement must be sent within **72 hours**
- Data export includes: account data, email history, contact records, usage logs
- Format: JSON or CSV, delivered via secure download link
- All Article 20 requests must be flagged to the Compliance team immediately
- Do NOT auto-reply with a generic response — legal acknowledgement required
- Internal compliance ticket must be created to track the 30-day deadline

### GDPR Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- A GDPR-compliant DPA is available to all customers upon request
- Enterprise customers receive a pre-signed DPA as part of onboarding
- DPA covers: data processing purposes, retention periods, sub-processor list, security measures
- Request via: legal@company.com

### Sub-processors
A current list of sub-processors is available at: company.com/sub-processors
Customers are notified 30 days before any new sub-processor is added.

### Data Residency
- Default: EU region (Frankfurt)
- US region available for Enterprise customers on request
- Data residency selection is locked at account creation; changes require migration (Enterprise only)

## HIPAA

### HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
- A HIPAA BAA is available to Enterprise customers handling Protected Health Information (PHI)
- BAA covers: permitted uses, safeguards, breach notification obligations
- Request via: compliance@company.com
- Setup time: 3–5 business days
- **HIPAA BAA is NOT available on Starter or Professional plans**

### PHI Handling
- Customers are responsible for ensuring they do not upload PHI unless a BAA is in place
- Our platform supports: encryption at rest (AES-256), encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), audit logging
- Breach notification: we notify affected Enterprise customers within 72 hours of a confirmed breach

## Security

### Ransomware / Extortion Threats
- Any email threatening data publication, ransomware, or extortion must be escalated to the Security team **immediately**
- Do NOT reply to the attacker under any circumstances
- Do NOT pay any ransom without executive + legal approval
- Escalation path: Security Team → CISO → CEO
- Preserve all evidence; do not delete the email

### Suspicious Login / Unauthorized Access
- Escalate to Security team immediately
- Notify the affected customer within 24 hours
- Force password reset on affected accounts
- Initiate incident response procedure

### Penetration Testing
- Customers may request permission to conduct penetration testing on their own instances
- Written approval required from Security team before any testing
- Unauthorized pen testing will trigger security incident response

## General Compliance

### Regulatory Audit Requests
- Formal regulatory audit requests (from government bodies, regulators) must be forwarded to legal@company.com immediately
- Do not respond directly to regulators without legal review

### Data Retention
- Customer data retained for 90 days after account cancellation, then permanently deleted
- Audit logs retained for 7 years (regulatory requirement)
- Customers may request early deletion via a GDPR Article 17 request
