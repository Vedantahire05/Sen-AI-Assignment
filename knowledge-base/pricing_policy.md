# Pricing Policy

## Subscription Tiers

### Starter Plan — $29/month
- Up to 3 team members, 1,000 emails/month
- Basic classification and sentiment tracking
- Community support only, no SLA guarantee

### Professional Plan — $99/month
- Up to 15 team members, 10,000 emails/month
- Full AI classification, RAG pipeline, agent automation
- Email support, 24h response SLA, 99.5% uptime SLA
- API access (500 requests/hour)

### Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing
- Unlimited team members and email volume
- Dedicated infrastructure option, 99.9% uptime SLA
- 24/7 phone + dedicated account manager
- HIPAA BAA, GDPR DPA, SOC 2 Type II included
- Minimum 12-month annual commitment

## Non-Profit Discount
- Registered 501(c)(3) organizations receive **30% off the Professional Plan**
- Verified at checkout; not applicable to Enterprise unless seats exceed 50

## Pro-Rata Billing on Upgrades

**Example:** Professional→Enterprise on day 10 of 30:
= ($499 - $99) × (20/30) = **$266.67**

Full new price applies from next billing cycle.

## Downgrade Policy
- Takes effect end of current billing cycle
- No refund for unused days on downgrade
- Feature access reverts immediately

## Annual Billing
- 2 months free with annual prepayment (~16.7% discount)
- Annual plans non-refundable after 14-day window

## Volume Discounts (Enterprise)
- 50–200 seats: 10% off
- 201–500 seats: 18% off
- 500+ seats: negotiated individually

## Price Change Policy
- 60-day notice before any increase
- Annual plan holders locked at contracted rate for full term
